Facilitator guide

Run of show, 45 minutes

This is a working session, not a lecture. Every block ends with students doing something on the handout. Put slides.html full screen on the projector and press P to open the presenter view on your laptop screen: notes, next slide, clock, run of show, and the student question board.

TimeBlockWhat you doAsk the room
12:30DoorsProjector on slide 1. Handout link and check-in QR on screen. Greet; ask each arriving student what job they want in two years."What's the job you'd want two years from now?"
0:00The countSlide 2. Start the 60-second timer. Students count people they know in the field. Poll Q1 on phones."Who got zero? That's most rooms. Good, you're in the right place."
0:03Why conversations winSlides 3–4. Roadmap in 30 seconds. LinkedIn 70% figure (2017 survey). Advice, not a job."Name one thing about your target job that no posting will tell you."
0:10The ask + the message shapeSlides 5–7. Two minutes of silent drafting (Step 2 on handout). Volunteer reads theirs; type it into the builder. Build one Why line out loud. Click the tab for majors in the room."Give me a person. Let's write the Why line together." "Could this message be sent to anyone? Then it goes to no one."
0:18Finding your fiveSlide 8. Run the dropdown with two students' answers. Open Illinois Tech Connect live if Wi-Fi allows; show the alumni filter. Poll Q2."Where will you find your first contact?"
0:26The conversation + sourcesSlides 9–10. Five moves in twenty minutes; the only ask is 'who else should I talk to.' Name the Elevate electives that count toward the checklist."Name one person you talked to this semester who works somewhere you'd want to work."
0:33Write one messageSlide 11. Start the 8-minute timer. Walk the room; push on the Why line. At 8:00 start the 3-minute swap."Partner: you're the recipient. Would you say yes? If not, why not?"
0:41Checklist + check-inSlides 12–13. Read the checklist fast. Leave the QR up. Students download or email the worksheet and book a one-on-one."Who are you messaging before Friday?"

If the room goes quiet

  • Pick a real alum from Illinois Tech Connect on screen and write the Why line yourself; ask the room only for the topic.
  • Ask for a show of hands instead of an answer; tap the tally so the screen moves.
  • Use the field tabs: "Who's in business? Here's yours."

Bring

  • Laptop and adapter, this site loaded (works offline once opened), handout link on the board.
  • Printed handouts for students without a device (handout.html prints cleanly).
  • Your own Illinois Tech Connect login, so you can demo the alumni filter live.
  • Sign-in: the 312 QR on slide 13 records attendance for Elevate credit.

After

  • Record attendance from 312 and log the session for Elevate workshop credit.
  • Reply to any emailed worksheets within two business days with one revision note each.
  • Export the Forms responses; clear the tallies on the poll page before the next session.

Edit the site
