Elevate experiences

Elevate is Illinois Tech's commitment that every student has access to hands-on experiences and mentorship. An Elevate experience is a structured, real-world activity that counts toward that commitment: research projects, internships and co-ops, study away, competitions, short courses, and similar hands-on opportunities.

The Who, Why, What message (copy it)

Subject: Illinois Tech student, 20 minutes on [topic]? Hi [Name], I'm a [year] [major] student at Illinois Tech. [One specific thing you noticed about their path, project, or post.] Could I have 20 minutes in the next two weeks to ask how you [the one topic]? Any time that suits you; I'm happy to work around your schedule. Thank you, [Your name]

Sample messages written by students: Santa Clara University · Loyola Marymount · MIT CAPD · The Muse.

The twenty-minute conversation

MinutesMove
0–2Thank them, restate the one topic, promise to stop at 20
2–8"How did you get from Illinois Tech to this job?" Then listen
8–16Your three questions a posting cannot answer
16–19"Who else should I talk to?" The only ask
19–20Stop on time. Thank-you the same day; update them in two weeks

Question banks: UC Berkeley Career Center · MIT CAPD · Kansas State · Elevate's own guide.
