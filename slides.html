0:13 · The message shape

Who. Why them. What you're asking.

W
Who. One line: year, major, Illinois Tech. No life story.
Y
Why them. Something specific you noticed: their path, a project, a post. Proof you did not mass-send.
A
Ask. Twenty minutes, one topic, in the next two weeks, at their convenience. Never a job, never a résumé review.
Ask the room

Call out a person you would message. We write their Why line together:

Why-line formulaI noticed their path, project, post, or talk, and why it matters to you.e.g. I saw your post about moving from QA into platform engineering, and that is the move I'm hoping to make.
It works in every major

Pick your field.

Formula for yoursWho: year + major. Why: one specific thing about them. Ask: 20 minutes on one topic.
