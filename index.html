Students will be able to

  1. Explain what an informational interview is and what it is not.
  2. Understand how to write a Who, Why, What outreach message in under 100 words.
  3. Build a target list of five people using Illinois Tech Connect and the LinkedIn alumni page.
  4. Research and develop their own informational interview question bank and plan a 20-minute conversation with three questions a job posting can’t answer.
