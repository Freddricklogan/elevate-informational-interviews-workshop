What you'll walk out able to do

  1. Explain what an informational interview is and what it is not (it is not a job ask).
  2. Write a Who, Why, What outreach message that gets a yes in under 100 words.
  3. Build a target list of five people using Illinois Tech Connect and the LinkedIn alumni page.
  4. Run a 20-minute conversation with three questions a job posting cannot answer.
  5. Follow up the same day and turn one conversation into the next introduction.
